Privacy Policy

Constant Clipz Bot · Effective October 8, 2026

Constant Clipz Bot ("the app") is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Constant Clipz YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

What the app accesses

The app uses the YouTube API Services with a single permission: uploading videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (the youtube.upload scope). It does not read, change or delete any other YouTube or Google account data.

What the app stores

  • The sign-in token Google issues so the app can upload videos. It is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never shared.
  • The clips the app prepares, their titles and descriptions, and a record of which ones were uploaded. These are also stored only on the owner's computer.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect personal information from anyone else.
  • It does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.
  • It does not use cookies or tracking.

YouTube and Google

By using YouTube features through this app, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove this app's access to your Google account at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.
